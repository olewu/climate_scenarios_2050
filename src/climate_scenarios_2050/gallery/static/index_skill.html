Index hindcast evaluation (evaluate-index), verified against the observed internal index. Skill per lead time: centred running means (evaluation.index_continuous), correlation or 1 − RMSE/std over all inits, with 95 % bootstrap bands. Window changes: change of the target-window mean against the 20 years before init. Hindcast: ensemble mean of the analogue hindcast by init and valid year, observed internal index below. Different indices are standardized differently and differ in persistence: compare each one's margin over its own AR(1) baselines and null test rather than the raw scores.
