Uncertainty partition of projected window changes

Spread of the CMIP6 projections of the change of an N-year window mean against the reference period before init, split into internal variability, model uncertainty and scenario uncertainty (Hawkins & Sutton 2009), by the centre year of the window (pixi run partition). Indices: from the forced/internal split of each scenario; targets: area mean of the target's CMIP6 field over a box (partition.regions). Each column is one experiment and quantity.
